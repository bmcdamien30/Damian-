The Truth Vocabulary

Three results. No hedging.

GarnettWork never converts uncertainty into a confident-sounding answer. If the evidence isn't there, it says so.

PASS

Identity and comparable market evidence support the asking price. This is a justified buy.

FAIL

Identity or evidence contradicts the listing: wrong model, inflated price, or a known risk pattern.

REFUSE

Evidence is insufficient to say either way. GarnettWork will not guess. It tells you what's missing.

What GarnettWork Checks

Five questions, answered honestly.

Market Evidence

What do genuinely comparable listings actually support, not a rough guess but a counted sample.

Max Safe Buy

The maximum price the evidence actually justifies for this item, in this condition, right now.

Receipt
